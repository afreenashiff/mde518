Study tips

Keep a small notebook as your household waste diary. Each evening, note how full your wet and dry bins are. You will need these notes for the field project.

Post one question or one photo in the study group every week. Learning from others is part of the course.

Before each live class, listen to the audio episode again and write down one doubt.
